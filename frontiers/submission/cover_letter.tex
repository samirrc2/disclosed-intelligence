\emph{Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence} --- AI in Finance

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent\textbf{Re: Original Research submission --- ``Disclosed Intelligence: A Large-Sample Measurement of AI Disclosure in U.S. Investment Adviser Fiduciary Filings.''}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit the above manuscript for consideration as an Original Research article in the AI in Finance specialty section of \emph{Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence}. The paper provides the first large-sample, independently validated measurement of how U.S. investment advisers disclose artificial intelligence in their Form ADV Part 2A brochures --- the client-facing narrative documents filed under the Uniform Application for Investment Adviser Registration (Form ADV) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). Drawing a stratified random sample of 400 firms from the Form ADV Part 1 population (16{,}223 advisers) and classifying 388 brochures with a prespecified five-category framework, we find that 23.7\% disclose some form of AI use and 27.6\% disclose AI-related risk, with disclosure rising with adviser size and more common among private-fund advisers. Beyond these estimates, the study contributes a validated approach to using large-language-model classification as a measurement instrument, established through blinded cross-family recoding ($\kappa = 0.74$) and design-aware statistical inference.

All analytical data, code, classification materials, and reproducibility resources are openly available in a published deterministic Code Ocean capsule (\url{https://doi.org/10.24433/CO.3404788.v1}), which regenerates every reported table, figure, and estimate. The study uses only publicly available regulatory data, makes no determination regarding legal compliance or misconduct by individual advisers, and discloses its use of generative AI assistance in the manuscript.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, has been approved by all authors, and the authors declare no competing interests. Thank you for your consideration.
